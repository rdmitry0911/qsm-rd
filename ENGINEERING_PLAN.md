# Historical engineering plan

The engineering plan formerly stored here targeted an experimental pre-QSM Direct stack and is superseded. Its assumptions, component names, and acceptance sequence must not be used for a current Proxmox VE deployment.

Current work is organized around the implemented QSM Direct browser console. The source of truth for its architecture, supported display profiles, encoder selection, and validation commands is [qsm-direct/README.md](qsm-direct/README.md).
