Hi guys, an update on text sharpness and input reliability. The signed APT repository now has stock4.3.

The earlier packet-loss fix did not cover genuine bandwidth changes: resetting the encoder could still change the quality of unchanged parts of the desktop, sometimes in horizontal bands. The worker now keeps its encoder settings and budgets pictures before encoding. A narrow connection reduces motion cadence rather than repeatedly changing the static background. Sudden large bandwidth drops can still cause recovery pauses.

In Firefox, controlled 8 -> 2 -> 8 Mbit/s target changes left the checked text region unchanged across 2111 frames. A separate Chromium test with an actual 20 -> 1 -> 20 Mbit/s connection recovered to 60 fps without reconnecting, with pauses during the abrupt drop. New checks cover NVENC H.264/HEVC and H.264 browser reception; I have not repeated Intel/AMD hardware qualification for this update.

There is also a more complete fix for repeated characters. Releasing keys on focus loss helped, but a delayed release could still let the guest start its own autorepeat. Exact typing is now the default: each press or real client repeat crosses as one complete action with its modifiers. The first character is immediate. In Firefox with a real LXC desktop and a 900 ms control-delivery gap, one short press gave 8 characters in the old mode and 1 in Exact typing. Six confirmed client events gave six characters.

Exact clicks extends this to mouse, trackpad taps/clicks and touchscreen taps. A short click is sent on release with its coordinates and modifiers. Moving 4 pixels with a button down or holding for 250 ms starts a drag/hold; drag movement and release stay ordered. Fractional trackpad scroll deltas are retained, including horizontal scrolling. The existing touchscreen hold-to-drag gesture stays the same.

Double click, right click, intentional holds, dragging and both scroll axes passed the browser/desktop tests. I have not qualified a physical trackpad yet. Physical holds and Raw input remain available when an application needs immediate held input. A connection stall can end a drag early, and native double-click timing can still be affected by delivery jitter.

The common VM/LXC paths are updated. The standalone profile still works with official PVE 9 packages, with no new guest agent requirement. Source, all five matching debs, guides and checks:
https://github.com/rdmitry0911/qsm-rd/releases/tag/v0.4.0-qsm-direct-20261024-stock4.3

For an existing APT installation, update all installed QSM profiles together:

apt update
apt install --only-upgrade qsm-pve-direct qsm-pve-direct-standalone qsm-pve-direct-lxc qsm-pve-direct-gateway

The --only-upgrade option skips uninstalled profiles. Reload the PVE page with cache bypass and reopen consoles. No VM/LXC reboot or desktop reprovisioning is needed. If you previously selected Raw, choose Guest first with Exact typing and Exact clicks to try these defaults.

Feedback would be welcome, especially from Firefox/VPN and trackpad users. Please mention VM or LXC, browser, encoder/codec, and direct UDP or the TCP 8006 gateway.

Thank you, Dmitry
