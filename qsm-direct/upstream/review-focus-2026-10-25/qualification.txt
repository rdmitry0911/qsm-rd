Stock4.5 focus/fullscreen regression, 2026-10-25

Stock4.4's blur/hidden handlers called releaseGuestKeyboardLock, which
explicitly called exitFullscreen. Temporary host app switching therefore
exited QSM fullscreen. Blur/hidden now suspends input and releases held
keys/buttons, retaining the existing fullscreen/capture intent. Focus/visible
resumes capture without restoring old holds or requesting fullscreen again.
Chromium unlocks while suspended and reacquires its separate lock on return.
Explicit Release, Settings, Client first and close still cancel/exit capture.
A fullscreen request completing while suspended cannot focus the background
video. Explicit cancellation still retires a late request.

Chromium, both standalone and embedded contexts. Injected window blur keeps
fullscreen and reports paused; injected focus resumes capture; explicit

handler deterministically, independently of automation's OS-focus quirks.
