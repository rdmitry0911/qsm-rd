The controls handle stays at the left screen edge when opening/closing the toolbar. Capture keyboard is now an enable/resume action: repeated clicks keep fullscreen and return input to the guest. Release keyboard is a separate Settings action; the fullscreen exit control also works in the embedded console. Settings still preserves fullscreen.

Firefox and Chromium checks pass at desktop and narrow sizes, both standalone and embedded. A headful Firefox/XTEST check confirms native Esc stays in the page after upgrading ordinary fullscreen to keyboard capture. All 22 CTest gates pass. No new physical Mac or hardware qualification is claimed; worker/bridge unchanged.

Reload PVE and reopen the console after upgrading. No guest update or restart is needed.
