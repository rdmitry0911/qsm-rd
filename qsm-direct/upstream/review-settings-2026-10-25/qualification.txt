Stock4.7 Settings/fullscreen/capture UI qualification, 2026-10-25

Root cause: updateGuestKeyboardLock grouped Settings with explicit Client
first, calling releaseGuestKeyboardLock / exitFullscreen. The text capture
action was below media/keyboard policy content, and an icon-only toolbar
control was hard to discover. A real click probe also found the midpoint
reveal handle intercepting Settings clicks in the embedded console.

Settings now releases held input, unlocks Chromium's separate controller
and preserves the native fullscreen capture session. DOM focus belongs to
Settings. Closing refocuses the video and resumes without a new fullscreen
request. Opening Settings while a request is pending retains capture intent
and cannot steal panel focus on completion. Explicit Release/Client first/
close and long Esc remain exit paths. Capture has visible text near the top
of the toolbar and is the first Settings action; Close is in the panel header.
Reopening resets scrollTop. A ResizeObserver keeps the reveal handle beside
the visible toolbar; hiding returns it to the screen edge. Observer disconnects
with console close. The embedded in-page maximize behavior is retained.

in Firefox 157 and Chromium, standalone and embedded. Native DOM clicks
verify capture without scrolling, no control/handle overlap, capture from
Settings, editing a local numeric setting while staying fullscreen, Close
refocus/resume, blur/focus preservation, and explicit Release exit. No page
errors. Screenshots included. Unit cases cover Firefox/Safari controller,
Chromium unlock/relock, explicit Client first, pending request/focus races.
