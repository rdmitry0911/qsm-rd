=================================

The user reported fewer interruptions with shared14, but remaining video
freezes/disappearances. Raising Firefox jitterBufferTarget to 120 ms made no
observable difference according to the user.

Path evidence from the PVE host to the reported VPN client, 10.90.10.21:
- The gateway returns ICMP fragmentation needed, MTU=1300, for a 1378-byte
  IPv4 probe with DF set.
- A 1300-byte DF IPv4 packet succeeds (~32 ms ICMP RTT).
- A 1301-byte DF IPv4 packet fails immediately with Message too long after
  the kernel learns the path MTU. No interface/router MTU was changed.

aiortc's existing H.264 packetizer allowed a 1300-byte RTP payload. The RTP
headers/extensions, SRTP tag, UDP and IP headers make the resulting datagram
larger than the observed path MTU. Complex/large video frames exercise the
full payload size more frequently. This is a concrete compatibility defect;
confirmation of the user's final playback experience remains outstanding.

shared15:
- QSM-specific H.264 single-NAL/FU-A packetization caps RTP payloads at 1150
  bytes; Annex-B parsing and codec behavior remain provided by aiortc.
- The optional HEVC packetizer uses the same payload budget.
- The change is scoped to QSM's encoder factory. The third-party aiortc
  module's global PACKET_MAX is not modified.
- Shared14's bounded reference-preserving queue and IDR recovery are retained.
- Codec, desktop frame rate, encoder bitrate, guest packages and compositor
  are unchanged. No resolution reduction or new network port is introduced.
